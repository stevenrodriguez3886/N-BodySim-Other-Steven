Solar system look + seed editor — pick your favorites

All variants keep the physics exactly as-is (invisible change only in how bodies are drawn). Stars are a fixed deterministic starfield, dimmed so bodies stay readable.

A · Flat & clean simplest

True planet colors + glow on the sun. Planets drawn at a guaranteed minimum pixel size (never sub-pixel), flat fill. Sun gets a soft radial glow. No shading, no rings — crisp and readable at any zoom.

B · Shaded + Saturn ring recommended

A with day/night shading (planets darken on the far side from the sun) + a ring for Saturn. Still cheap to draw: one inner shadow per planet, one ellipse for the ring. Reads instantly as "planets".

C · Banded gas giants max flavor

B + horizontal bands on Jupiter/Saturn (and faint ones on Uranus/Neptune), drawn as 2–3 clipped stripes. Most realistic, slightly busiest at small sizes.
